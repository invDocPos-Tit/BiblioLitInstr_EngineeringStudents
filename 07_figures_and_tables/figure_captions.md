# Figure captions (list required by EJEE after the figures)

Translated from the captions in `manuscrito/construir_manuscrito.py` (Spanish manuscript, 2026-11-22). Same figures and data; titles are not drawn inside the images.

**Figure 1.** PRISMA 2020 flow diagram of record selection. A2 (period) and A3 (language) share one exclusion code. A7 (original empirical evaluation of a measurement property) is a criterion of the systematic review that shared the screening and is not required here. Uncertain records took the full-text eligibility decision of that review.

**Figure 2.** Annual production by document type (n = 347). 2026 (*) is a partial year because the search was run in November 2026.

**Figure 3.** Terms of the search-string blocks in titles, abstracts and author keywords (n = 347). Panel (a): documents naming each competency family (orange = technical, blue = transversal); the dark segment is the number of those documents that also name performance or observation instrument terms (rubric, concept inventory, performance-based task or situational judgement). Panel (b): percentage of journal articles and conference papers using each measurement-property term family, with 95% bootstrap intervals. Families are defined in Supplementary material D; a document may belong to several.

**Figure 4.** Co-citation network of the 24 works most cited by the corpus. Node size = number of corpus documents citing the work; links join works co-cited in at least three documents; colours = communities within this subset; grey, bottom row = works without links at that threshold. Labels follow the OpenAlex authorship of each record. † Same book with two records in OpenAlex. ‡ OpenAlex record of a book review whose title matches Kline's textbook (see Table 1). § OpenAlex record without authors, labelled by its title. The network includes works not cited in the text, which are therefore not in the reference list.

**Figure 5.** Documents per country. Panel a: the 15 leading countries (full counting: a document counts once for each country; blue = OpenAlex affiliations, orange = manual search). Panel b: documents per country on a logarithmic scale.

**Figure 6.** Collaboration between countries (36 countries with links; 48 pairs). Only documents with OpenAlex affiliations (n = 298). Node size = documents of the country; edge width = co-authored documents (legend).

**Figure 7.** Field-normalised citation impact by document subject field: field-weighted citation impact (OpenAlex FWCI, world average 1.0; 256 documents) by OpenAlex document subject field, for fields with at least 5 documents. Bars = median (blue ≥ 1, red < 1), diamonds = mean; labels give median / mean (n).

**Figure S1.** The 30 terms with the highest co-occurrence strength, ordered by mean publication year. Size = total link strength. Terms come from the curated author-keyword model (n = 313 documents; 2,338 canonical terms). 43.8% of the corpus dates from 2025–2026, which shifts the means towards recent years.

**Figure S2.** Strategic thematic map (6 curated author-keyword clusters). Dashed lines are the medians of centrality and density. Bubble size = number of keywords in the cluster.

**Figure S3.** Deduplicated reference publication year spectroscopy (RPYS; Marx et al., 2014) across 10,842 unique document–work citations (1951–2026), with an inset for 1950–1984 highlighting single-work historical roots (≥ 50% annual citation concentration, orange markers) alongside broad volume waves (≤ 4% concentration, blue markers); shaded band = incomplete citation window (2024–2026).
